## Staging acceptance flows

1. Patient registers/verifies, books an available slot, pays in provider test mode, joins only in the allowed time window, sees their own encounter record and cannot access another patient's data.
2. Practitioner records an encounter and amendment, creates a care plan and completes sessions without exceeding the plan. Reception/admin remain unable to read clinical notes.
3. Student applies, is approved, pays, consumes the pinned curriculum, attends classes, submits an assignment and quiz, then receives a certificate only when every gate passes. Revocation must be reflected in verification.
4. Vendor applies, enrolls MFA, gets approved, submits products and stock, then handles only its own paid order items. Concurrent checkout must never oversell. Commission snapshots must match approved terms.
5. Finance handles duplicate captures, late captures, full refunds and provider timeouts without duplicate money postings or stock returns. Confirm provider statements against the ledger.
6. Admin manages staff roles, responds to support/privacy requests and reviews audit events. Verify deactivated users and revoked sessions lose access immediately.
7. Run the same supported patient/student journeys on Android, including network interruption, app restart, expired authentication and denied notification permission.
